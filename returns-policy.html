RETURNS & REFUNDS

Last updated: September 15, 2026

NO RETURNS OR REFUNDS

Due to the handmade and custom nature of our products, we do not accept returns, exchanges, or refunds. All sales are final.

WHY SALES ARE FINAL

  • Each item is made to order with care using premium soy wax and fragrance oils.
  • We thoroughly inspect every order before shipping to ensure quality.
  • Slight variations in color, shape, or fill level are normal and part of the handmade process.
  • Once an item leaves our studio, we cannot accept returns for hygiene and safety reasons. Melted wax products cannot be resold.

EXCEPTIONS

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If your item arrives damaged or significantly different from what you ordered, such as the wrong scent or a broken bottle, contact us with photos within 48 hours of delivery. We will review your case and may offer a replacement or refund at our discretion.

BEFORE PURCHASING

  • Read the full item description carefully.
  • Check all listing photos.
  • Message us with any questions before buying.

By completing your purchase, you agree to this No Returns or Refunds Policy.

CONTACT

Email Support@sipofghoulaid.com with your order number and photos if you need help with a damaged or incorrect item.
