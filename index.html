Custom orders require extensive behind-the-scenes work. Because each order is unique and requires custom mold creation or silicone-reusable handwork, all orders are subject to discussion with you before we begin. ABOUT SIP OF GHOULAID

Hi, I'm the ghoul behind the Ghoulaid.

Sip of Ghoulaid was born from my love of all things spooky, weird, creative, and a little unhinged—and the inability to leave a perfectly strange idea living only inside my head.

What started with creativity and a love for making things has turned into my own little spooky laboratory, where I get to design, experiment, and bring creepy-cute creations to life.

A lot of what you see here starts completely from scratch. I design pieces, 3D print masters, create molds, hand-pour wax, and hand-paint the tiny details that give each creation its personality.

I especially love creating things that make you stop and think:

"Okay... that's weird. I need it."

And if the thing living inside your head doesn't exist yet? That's where custom orders come in. Give me your strange, spooky, adorable, or completely chaotic idea, and I'll see what kind of monster we can create together.

Why Sip of Ghoulaid?

Sip of Ghoulaid isn't about perfectly mass-produced products.

It's about handmade weirdness, creativity, personality, and making things that are a little different from everything else.

Thanks for supporting my little spooky corner of the world and allowing me to keep making weird shit.

Stay spooky. Stay weird.

And remember...

DON'T DRINK THE GHOULAID.
