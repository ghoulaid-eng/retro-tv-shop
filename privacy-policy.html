PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: April 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sip of Ghoulaid collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you shop on my Big Cartel site or contact me. I am a small handmade business based in Southern California, United States.

INFORMATION I COLLECT

When you place an order, I receive:

  • Your name
  • Shipping address
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Order details

If you message me, I collect your name, email, and message content. I do not collect or store payment information—payments are processed securely by Big Cartel or the payment provider.

HOW I USE YOUR INFORMATION

I use your information to:

  • Process and ship your orders
  • Send shipping updates and respond to your messages
  • Provide customer support
  • Meet legal and tax record-keeping requirements

SHARING YOUR INFORMATION

I do not sell or rent your information. I may share it only as needed with:

  • Shipping carriers, such as USPS or UPS, to deliver your package
  • Big Cartel and payment processors to complete transactions
  • Legal authorities if required by law

DATA RETENTION

I keep order information only as long as needed to fulfill orders, provide support, and comply with tax laws—usually 3–7 years—then securely delete or anonymize it.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting me. California residents may have additional rights under state law.

SECURITY

I take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no online method is 100% secure.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

I may update this policy occasionally. The “Last Updated” date shows the latest version.
