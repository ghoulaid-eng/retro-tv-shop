SHIPPING POLICY

Last updated: April 17, 2026

Thank you for shopping with Sip of Ghoulaid!

PROCESSING TIME

All orders are handmade to order. Please allow 1–3 business days for processing and preparation before your order ships. During busy periods or holidays, processing may take up to 5 business days. We will notify you if there is any delay.

SHIPPING METHODS & COSTS

We ship within the United States via USPS (United States Postal Service).

  • Shipping cost is calculated at checkout based on your location and package weight.
  • We offer standard shipping. Expedited or priority options may be available at checkout.

SHIPPING TIMES

Once shipped, estimated delivery times are:

  • Standard shipping: 3–7 business days, not guaranteed.

Shipping times are estimates only and can be affected by carrier delays, weather, holidays, or high shipping volume. We are not responsible for delays caused by the shipping carrier.

ORDER TRACKING

You will receive a tracking number via email once your package ships. You can track your order through the link provided or on the USPS website.

INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING

We do not offer international shipping at this time.

ADDRESS ACCURACY

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We are not responsible for orders shipped to an incorrect or incomplete address. If an order is returned to us due to an incorrect address, you will be responsible for reshipping costs or we can issue a refund minus original shipping fees.

DAMAGED OR LOST PACKAGES

If your package arrives damaged, please contact us with photos within 48 hours of delivery.

If your package is lost by the carrier, we will work with you and the carrier to resolve the issue.
